# Beyond Survival – storyboard (14 escenas)
Estilo común: cinematic photograph, 35mm film, soft film grain, muted moody palette, shallow depth of field, no people, no text, lower third of frame darker and uncluttered (space for subtitles), vertical 9:16.

1. Hell and back / know the path — narrow stone path through dark volcanic landscape, faint glowing lava cracks, smoke.
2. Something in us transforms / not fragile — glowing coals and embers rising in darkness.
3. Burnt / rebuild from the ashes — burned forest at dusk, ash falling softly.
4. Scars with a purpose — kintsugi ceramic bowl with gold seams on dark wood, single warm light.
5. Wounded is not fragile — charred tree trunk still standing, cracked bark, faint new growth.
6. Sometimes suffering simply hurts — rain on a dark window at night, blurred cold lights.
7. Resilience = transform damage — tiny green sprout emerging from black ash, macro.
8. Hell didn't take our ability to live — wildflowers blooming across a burned hillside.
9. We don't always choose the fire — distant wildfire glow on a night ridge.
10. Choose what to do with what remains — misty forest path splitting in two.
11. Scars = evidence of a life that continued — old tree with a burn scar, full green canopy, sunbeams.
12. Destruction doesn't have the final word — calm lake at dawn, mist.
13. Don't have to walk it again — path leading away from a distant smoking mountain toward a green valley.
14. Who do we get to become / Freedom — open horizon, golden sunrise over a meadow, birds in flight.
